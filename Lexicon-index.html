Architecture Principles

Human-in-the-Loop

No contract is auto-approved above configurable risk thresholds. Critical severity clauses always force senior attorney review regardless of overall score.

Confidence Gating

AI outputs below the confidence floor are always routed to human review. The system treats AI as probabilistic, never deterministic.

Deterministic Rules Engine

Business policy constraints override AI recommendations. Configurable thresholds ensure regulatory compliance regardless of model output.

Prompt Version Control

Multiple prompt strategies tracked with evaluation scoring. Every analysis is tied to a specific prompt version for reproducibility.

Design Rationale

Why Structured Intake

In enterprise legal operations, the #1 failure point for automation is unstructured input. This module ensures every contract enters the pipeline with metadata that enables deterministic routing independent of AI output.

Metadata-Driven Routing

Document type, urgency, and business unit feed directly into the rules engine. A low-risk NDA for Corporate follows a different path than a high-risk MSA for M&A.

Chain of Custody

The submission event is logged before any AI processing occurs, creating a traceable audit trail from the first touch — mapping to matter number, responsible attorney, and client code.

💡 Enterprise Note: In production, this integrates with the firm's DMS (iManage, NetDocuments) and matter management platform.
